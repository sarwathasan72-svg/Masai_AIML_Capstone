# Masai_AIML_Capstone
Masai Capstone - Delhivery Last-Mile Delay Intelligence Pipeline

## Data findings
-Service type: Express had the highest delay rate, 48.97%, compared with Standard 20.56% and Economy 8.57%. Express has the shortest delivery promise: 36 hours.
-Hub tier: Delay rates increased from 4.00% for Tier 1 to 21.50% for Tier 2 and 44.00% for Tier 3.
-Distance: Delay rates increased across the four distance bands: 0–200 km: 2.19%; 201–400 km: 13.68%; 401–600 km: 43.64%; above 600 km: 85.23%..
-Correlations: The three strongest absolute correlations with delivery_hours were promised_hours (+0.8372), distance_km (+0.4013), and n_stops (+0.3247).
-Declared value: After filling missing values, the mean was INR 1,533.05, above the median of INR 1,121.50, suggesting right skew. After the log transformation, the mean (7.0277) and median (7.0224) were much closer. The transformation compresses large values and reduces skew; log values are not amounts in INR.
